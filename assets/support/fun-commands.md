Two commands are purely for fun.

### /fun
Dad jokes, ASCII art, fake tweets, a fake hack, an IQ test, a fake Nitro gift, Master Oogway's wisdom, a Pepe sign and a relationship checker.

### /how
Measures how sus, smart or simp somebody is, among others. The results are definitely accurate.

Each one also works as its own prefix command, such as `{prefix}dadjoke` or `{prefix}ship`.
