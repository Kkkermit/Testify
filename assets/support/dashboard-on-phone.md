The dashboard works on phones and tablets as well as computers.

- On a narrow screen the sidebar folds into a menu. Tap the **menu button** at the top left to open it.
- The name of the server you are changing sits above every screen, so you always know which one you are in.
- Wide tables scroll sideways inside their card rather than stretching the page.
