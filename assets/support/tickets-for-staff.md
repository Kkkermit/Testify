Every ticket channel starts with a message holding the controls.

- **Claim** marks the ticket as yours, so other staff know it is handled.
- **Lock** stops the member writing, and **Unlock** lets them write again.
- **Members** adds or removes another member from the ticket.
- **Close** posts a transcript to the transcripts channel and deletes the ticket after {fact:ticket.closeDelay}.

Only the staff role can claim, lock or add members. The member who opened the ticket can close it too.

> **Tip:** A member can have one ticket open at a time, so they are asked to close it before opening another.
