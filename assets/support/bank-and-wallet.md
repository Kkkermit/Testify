Your money sits in two places.

- **Wallet**: what you spend from, and what robbers can take.
- **Bank**: safe from robbers. Passive income from houses and businesses lands here.

### Moving money
- `/deposit` moves money into the bank, and `/withdraw` moves it back. Give an amount or `all`, or leave it out and pick from buttons.
- `/transfer` sends money from your wallet to another member.

`/balance` shows both, for you or for someone else.
