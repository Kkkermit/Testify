title: Running the lottery
keywords: lottery, tickets pot, draw, jackpot, raffle, lotto
---
The lottery is a pot that members buy tickets into, drawn on a schedule.

- `/lottery setup` starts one, `/lottery enter` buys a ticket and `/lottery info` shows the pot and when it is drawn.
- `/lottery freeze` pauses ticket sales and `/lottery delete` ends it.
- On the dashboard, open your server and choose **Lottery** under Economy.
