Everybody with **Manage Server** or **Administrator**, and the server's owner, sees every screen. The owner and admins can give anybody else some of the screens, by user ID, from the server's **Dashboard access** screen in the sidebar.

### Give somebody access
1. In Discord, turn on Developer Mode, right-click them and choose **Copy User ID**.
2. On **Dashboard access**, paste the ID, tick the screens they need, and choose **Give access**. A section's own box ticks every screen in it.
3. They sign in to {bot}'s dashboard and find the server under **Shared with you**.

### What a screen gives them
- It stands in for Manage Server on that screen only, so a moderation screen can be shared without handing out any settings.
- Kicking, banning, timeouts and handing out roles still need that permission in Discord, exactly as the commands do.
- The access screen itself is never shared.

### Keep track of it
Open anybody on the list to see who gave them access and when, when they were last there, and what they changed. Change their screens or take their access away from the same page. Leaving the server ends their access too.
