The player starts at 50%. Change it with `/music volume` and a percentage, or with the volume buttons on the player panel.

A change takes a moment to be heard, because the track is reopened at the new level from where it was.

> **Tip:** Volume control needs FFmpeg on the machine hosting the bot. Without it the player stays at the track's own level and says so. `/music status` shows what the host has installed.
