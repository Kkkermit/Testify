The moderation commands each need the matching Discord permission.

- `/ban`, `/unban`, `/kick`, and `/softban` for a ban that lifts itself.
- `/mute` times a member out and `/unmute` lifts it.
- `/warn` issues warnings, and lists, edits or clears them.
- `/clear` deletes recent messages in bulk, and `/lock` and `/unlock` close and reopen a channel.

On the dashboard, open your server and choose **Leaderboards**, then a member, to see their warnings and moderate them from one page.
