`/game` holds the games.

- `/game blackjack`: a hand against the dealer, with a bet.
- `/game 8ball`: ask the magic eight ball a question.
- `/game fast-type`: race to type a sentence correctly.
- `/game guess-the-number`: guess the number the bot picked.
- `/game guess-the-pokemon`: name the Pokémon from its artwork.
- `/game rps`: rock, paper, scissors.
- `/game would-you-rather`: a would-you-rather question.

Most have a short prefix alias too, such as `{prefix}bj` or `{prefix}wyr`.
