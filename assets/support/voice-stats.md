{bot} can rename voice channels to show how many members and bots your server has.

- **In Discord:** run `/voice-stats` and pick a channel for the member counter, the bot counter, or both.
- **On the dashboard:** open your server's **Settings** and find **Member count channels**.

> **Tip:** Discord lets a channel be renamed only twice every ten minutes, so the counts can lag a little behind.
