Both need **Manage Channels**.

### Locking
- `/lock` stops everyone from sending messages in a channel. It defaults to the one you are in, and takes an optional reason.
- `/unlock` lets them talk again.

### Slowmode
`/slowmode` sets how long each member waits between messages, such as `10s` or `5m`. Use `0` to turn it off.

### On the dashboard
Open your server, then **Channel tools** under Moderation. Pick a channel to lock it or set its slowmode, and the list underneath shows every channel that is locked or slowed right now.
