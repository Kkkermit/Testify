title: Setting up tickets
keywords: tickets, ticket, support ticket, contact staff, help desk, ticket panel, transcripts, open ticket
---
Tickets give members a button that opens a private channel with your staff.

- In Discord, run `/ticket setup`. It asks for the channel to post the panel in, the category new tickets are created in, where transcripts go, and the staff role that handles tickets.
- On the dashboard, open your server and choose **Tickets**, then publish the panel.

`/ticket status` shows the current setup. `/ticket disable` turns tickets off and leaves existing ticket channels alone.
