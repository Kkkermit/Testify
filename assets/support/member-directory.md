Open your server, then **Members**. The **Directory** tab lists everybody in the server, read live from Discord a page at a time.

### Finding somebody
Search by name, nickname or Discord ID, and filter by role, by when they joined, by people or bots and by who is timed out. With the Moderate Members permission you also see warning counts, and can filter by who has warnings.

### Acting from a row
Each row has **Actions**, which offers only what you could do in Discord: give or take a role you could hand out yourself, time out, warn, kick or ban. Somebody whose highest role is at or above yours says so instead.

### Acting on several at once
Tick up to {fact:directory.maxBatch} members, then choose what to do.
- They are acted on one at a time, and you are told what happened to each. Anybody who cannot be acted on is skipped and named.
- You, the server's owner and {bot} are never acted on, even if ticked.
- A kick or ban of several asks you to type how many you ticked. Each of them gets a case of their own.
