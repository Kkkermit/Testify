id: remove-the-bot
title: Removing the bot from a server
keywords: remove bot, kick bot, uninstall, leave server, get rid of bot
---
Anybody with **Kick Members** can remove the bot: right-click it in the member list and choose **Kick**.

If you only want to stop part of it, switch that feature off or turn individual commands off under **Commands** on the dashboard instead.
