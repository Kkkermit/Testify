A greeting can take one of three styles:

- **Text**: an ordinary message in the channel.
- **Embed**: a coloured panel, easier to spot in a busy channel.
- **Card**: a drawn image with the member's avatar, plus the message.

### Changing the card's background
Run `/welcome background` and attach a PNG or JPG. Around 1024×400 looks best, and the file can be up to {fact:welcome.maxBackgroundMb}. The panel's **Remove background** button goes back to the default.

> **Tip:** The bot needs **Attach Files** in the welcome channel to post the card.
