`/gamble` bets money from your wallet on a game of chance.

- **Coinflip**: pick heads or tails. Win and you get double your bet back.
- **Dice**: you and the house each roll. Roll higher and you get double back.
- **Slots**: three matching symbols pay five times your bet, and two matching pay one and a half times.

Lose and the bet is gone. `/game blackjack` is another way to bet, against a dealer.

> **Tip:** Only bet what is in your wallet and you are happy to lose.
