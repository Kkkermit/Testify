`/leaderboard economy` shows the ten richest people, and `/leaderboard levels` the ten highest levels in this server.

- If you are not in the top ten, your own rank is shown underneath.
- Buttons on the money board switch between **This server** and **Every server**, which adds up each person's money in every server {bot} is in, and between ranking by **Total**, **Wallet** or **Bank**. Only the person who asked can press them.
- **scope** and **sort** choose where the money board starts.

`/rank` shows your own level, XP and position as an image card, or someone else's if you name them.

On the dashboard, open your server, then **Leaderboards** under Community to see both as tables.
