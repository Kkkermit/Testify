`/leaderboard economy` shows the richest people, and `/leaderboard levels` the highest levels in this server.

- **sort** ranks the money board by **Total**, **Wallet** or **Bank**.
- **scope** chooses **This server**, or **Every server**, which adds up each person's money in every server {bot} is in.
- **page** picks a page, and the line under the board says where you sit and what to type for the next one.

`/rank` shows your own level, XP and position as an image card, or someone else's if you name them.

On the dashboard, open your server, then **Leaderboards** under Community to see both as tables.
