If an article does not answer your question, there are three more places to look.

1. **`/help`** in Discord lists every command, category by category, with the options each one takes.
2. **The [command list](/commands)** on the dashboard is searchable by name or alias.
3. **The support server**, linked at the bottom of the [Help page](/help) and under every `/ask` answer, is where to reach a person.

> **Tip:** When you ask a question here, try the words you would search for, like "welcome image" or "level roles". Suggestions appear as you type.
