{bot} is an all-in-one Discord bot. One bot covers what most servers need, and you switch on only the parts you want.

### What it can do
- **Community:** welcome messages, levelling with role rewards, leaderboards and counting.
- **Moderation:** warnings, timeouts, bans and softbans, AutoMod filters, link filtering and an audit log.
- **Server tools:** tickets, verification, roles on join, sticky messages and member count channels.
- **Economy:** money, a shop, jobs, pets, a lottery, giveaways and random treasure drops.
- **Entertainment:** music from YouTube and SoundCloud, games, and plenty of fun commands.

### Two ways to use it
- **In Discord**, with slash commands like `/help`, or prefix commands like `{prefix}help`.
- **On this dashboard**, where server managers can set everything up with a click.

> **Tip:** Nothing is switched on by default, so a new server stays quiet until you choose what it does.
