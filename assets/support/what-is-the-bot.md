{bot} is a multi-purpose Discord bot. One bot covers levelling, welcome messages, moderation, audit logging, tickets, verification, an economy with a shop and pets, giveaways, a lottery, music, games and plenty of fun commands.

Nothing is switched on by default. Add the bot to a server, then turn on only the parts you want, either with commands in Discord or from this dashboard.

- Members use it in Discord, through slash commands and prefix commands.
- Server managers can also configure it from the dashboard.
- [Browse every command](/commands) to see what it can do.
