Link filtering deletes links posted by anybody without the **bypass permission**.

### Setting it up
- **In Discord:** run `/anti-link`, turn it on, and choose the bypass permission.
- **On the dashboard:** open your server's **Settings** and find **Link filtering**.

### The bypass permission
Members holding it may still post links. Choose from Manage Messages, which covers most moderators, Manage Server, Timeout Members or Administrator.

> **Tip:** Every removed link is recorded as a warning, so repeat offenders show up in `/warn list`.
