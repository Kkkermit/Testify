AutoMod rules are Discord’s own message filters, and the bot sets them up for you.

- In Discord, `/automod` creates rules for flagged words, spam, mention spam or your own keywords. `/automod list` shows them and `/automod delete` removes one.
- On the dashboard, open your server and choose **AutoMod**.

Because these are Discord’s own rules, they keep working even if the bot is offline, and you can also see them under **Server Settings → AutoMod** in Discord.
