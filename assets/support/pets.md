Adopt a pet from the **Pets** section of `/shop`, or browse them with `/pet shop`. Pets come in five rarities, from common to legendary.

### Looking after it
- `/pet view` shows its mood, hunger and happiness.
- `/pet feed` feeds it. Every pet has its own feeding cost.
- `/pet walk` takes it out and cheers it up.

Hunger and happiness drift over time, so check on it now and then.

### Other commands
- `/pet rename` gives it a name of your own.
- `/pet rehome` gives it up for half of what you paid.
