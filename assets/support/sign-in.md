The dashboard signs you in with Discord, so there is no separate password.

### Signing in
1. Press **Sign in with Discord**.
2. Discord asks you to approve the dashboard. It receives your user ID, username, avatar and the list of servers you are in, never your password or email.
3. You land back on the page you were trying to open.

### Signing out
Use **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar. The session ends straight away.

> **Tip:** Being sent to the sign-in page unexpectedly means the session expired. Sign in again and you return to where you were.
