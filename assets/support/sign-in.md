title: Signing in to the dashboard
keywords: sign in, log in, login, logging in, account, oauth, authorise, sign out, log out, logout
---
The dashboard signs you in with Discord, so there is no separate password. Discord’s own page asks you to approve the sign-in, and the dashboard receives your user ID, username, avatar and the list of servers you are in. It never sees your Discord password or email.

To sign out, use **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar. Signing out ends that session straight away.

If you are sent back to the sign-in page unexpectedly, your session has expired. Sign in again and you return to the page you were on.
