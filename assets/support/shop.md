`/shop` opens the shop. Each section has a **Buy** button beside every entry, and the money comes out of your wallet.

### Sections
- **Items**: tools such as a fishing rod or hunting rifle, which you use for money, a laptop that some jobs need, and a padlock that protects your wallet from one robbery.
- **Houses** and **Businesses**: pay passive income into your bank every hour.
- **Jobs**: pick the job that `/work` pays you for.
- **Pets**: adopt a pet to look after.

### Using what you own
`/inventory` lists your items with a **Use** button beside each one that can be used. You can carry up to {fact:economy.inventoryMax} items.
