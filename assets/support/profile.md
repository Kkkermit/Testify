Your profile is about you rather than a server, so it follows you everywhere the bot is.

- `/profile set` fills it in: a line about you and a favourite song, plus a favourite game, hobbies and birthday if you like.
- `/profile view` shows yours, or someone else's.
- `/profile delete` removes it.
