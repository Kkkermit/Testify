Each server has its own economy, so money earned in one server stays there.

1. Run `/economy create` to open an account.
2. Earn with `/daily`, `/work` and `/beg`.
3. Check your money with `/balance`, and move it with `/deposit` and `/withdraw`.
4. Spend it in `/shop`, and use what you own from `/inventory`.

Pets are adopted from the shop and looked after with `/pet`. `/leaderboard` shows the richest members.
