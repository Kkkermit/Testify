The bot's owner has blocked your account from every command, in every server. The message gives the reason they recorded.

The usual reasons are in the [terms of use](/terms):
- Spamming commands or buttons far more than using them needs.
- Using scripts or several accounts to farm rewards or get round a cooldown.
- Exploiting a bug instead of reporting it.

Server staff cannot lift it. If you think it is a mistake, ask in the support server linked from the [Help page](/help).
