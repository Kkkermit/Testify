An idea board is a channel where members post ideas for your server and everybody votes on them.

### Setting it up
Run `/idea settings`, pick the channel ideas go to, and turn it on. You need **Manage Server**. On the dashboard it is **Idea board** under Community.

### Posting and voting
- Members post with `/idea submit`. Each idea gets a number and its own post in the board's channel.
- Anybody votes with 👍 or 👎 on the post. Pressing the same one again takes the vote back.
- A member can post one idea a minute.

### Deciding
Staff with **Manage Server** press **Accept** or **Decline** on the post and can give a reason. Voting closes, the post shows the decision, and the author is told by DM. **Reopen** undoes it. The dashboard does the same, and can delete an idea.

> **Tip:** `/idea` is for your own server. `/suggest` is different: it sends a suggestion about {bot} itself to its developers.
