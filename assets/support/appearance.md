Open **Appearance** at the bottom of the sidebar.

- **Theme**: light, dark, or match your device.
- **Accent colour**: the colour of buttons and highlights.
- **Motion**: follow your device, always animate, or hold everything still.
- **Language**: English, Spanish, German, French, Italian or Russian.

Your choices are saved in this browser and affect nobody else.
