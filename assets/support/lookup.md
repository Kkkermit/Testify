`/lookup` fetches things from outside Discord.

- `/lookup translate`: translate text into one of 15 languages.
- `/lookup wiki`: a Wikipedia summary.
- `/lookup meme`: a random meme from Reddit.
- `/lookup advice`: a piece of random advice.
- `/lookup animal-fact`: a fact and a photo of an animal.
- `/lookup minecraft`: whether a Minecraft server is online.

Each also works as its own prefix command, such as `{prefix}translate` or `{prefix}wiki`.
