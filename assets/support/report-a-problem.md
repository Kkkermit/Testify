Two commands send feedback straight to the people who run the bot.

- `/bug-report` asks for a short summary and the details. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
- `/suggest` sends an idea.

Each can be used once a minute.

> **Tip:** For a quick answer from a person, use the support server linked at the bottom of the [Help page](/help).
