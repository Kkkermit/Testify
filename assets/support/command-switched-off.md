Somebody turned that command off.

- **“Switched off in this server”**: anybody with **Manage Server** can turn it back on, under **Commands** on the dashboard.
- **“This command is under maintenance”**: the bot's owner has turned it off everywhere for now. It may come back later, and only they can turn it back on.
