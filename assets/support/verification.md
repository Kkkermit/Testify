Verification makes new members prove they are a person before they get a role: they press a button, type a short code, and the role is theirs.

- In Discord, run `/verify setup` to open the verification panel. `/verify edit` changes it and `/verify disable` turns it off. These need **Manage Server**.
- On the dashboard, open your server’s **Settings** and find **Verification**.

The bot needs **Manage Roles**, and its role must sit above the role it gives.
