- `/user-info`: when someone's account was made, when they joined, their nickname and their highest role.
- `/avatar`: someone's avatar and banner, full size.
- `/server-info`: details about this server.
- `/role-info`: details about one role.
- `/member-count`: a chart of the members, as bars, a pie or a doughnut.
- `/permissions`: the permissions a member has here.
- `/bot`: information about {bot} itself, how long it has been up and what it runs on.
