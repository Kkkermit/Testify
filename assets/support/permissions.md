Configuring the bot needs the **Manage Server** permission, whether you do it in Discord or on the dashboard. The dashboard checks it again on every request, so somebody who loses the permission loses access straight away.

Moderation commands need the matching Discord permission: `/ban` needs Ban Members, `/kick` needs Kick Members, `/mute` needs Moderate Members, and so on.

The bot also needs its own permissions to act. If it cannot do something, the reply names the permission it is missing.
