**Overview** is the first page for each server.

- **Stat tiles** show the member, channel and role counts.
- **Features** lists what the bot is running here, with a link to each one's screen.
- **Testify is missing permissions** appears when a feature is set up but the bot lacks a permission it needs. Those features quietly do nothing until the permission is given.
- **Recent changes** lists every change made from the dashboard: who made it, what changed and when.
