A ban appeal lets somebody you banned ask to come back, without needing a way to reach your staff first.

### Setting it up
Run `/appeals`, pick a channel only staff can see, and turn appeals on. You need **Manage Server**. On the dashboard it is **Appeals** under Moderation. Appeals are written on the dashboard, so they only work while {bot} runs one.

### Appealing
Banned members open the [Appeal a ban](/appeals) page and sign in with Discord. The message {bot} sends with a ban links there. The page lists every server that has banned them and takes appeals, and each takes one appeal at a time.

### Deciding
Each appeal is posted to the channel you picked, with **Accept** and **Refuse**. Either needs **Ban Members**. Accepting lifts the ban. Refusing needs a reason, and after a refusal the member waits before appealing again. You choose how long, from a week to three months. They are told your decision and reason by DM. The dashboard lists every appeal and can decide them too.

> **Tip:** An appeal never pings anybody, whatever it says.
