1. **Is levelling switched on?** Check **Levelling** on the dashboard, or run `/levelling setup`.
2. **Is the channel or role ignored?** Ignored channels and roles earn nothing.
3. **Is it the cooldown?** After earning XP, a member waits {fact:levelling.xpCooldown} before their messages count again, however fast they type.

> **Tip:** `/rank` shows exactly how much XP someone has, and how much the next level needs.
