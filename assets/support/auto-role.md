{bot} can hand one or more roles to everybody the moment they join.

- **In Discord:** run `/auto-role` and tick the roles in the panel.
- **On the dashboard:** open your server's **Settings** and find **Roles on join**.

What is ticked is what new members get. Deselect a role to stop giving it.

> **Tip:** The bot's role must sit above every role it hands out, and roles managed by an integration cannot be given at all.
