id: auto-role
title: Giving roles to new members automatically
keywords: auto role, autorole, join role, roles on join, default role, give role on join, automatic role
---
The bot can give one or more roles to everybody who joins.

- In Discord, run `/auto-role`.
- On the dashboard, open your server’s **Settings** and find **Roles on join**.

The bot’s own role must sit above every role it hands out, or Discord refuses.
