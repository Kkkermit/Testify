- `/add emoji` copies an emoji from another server into yours. Paste the emoji and give it a name.
- `/add sticker` adds a sticker, with a name and optional tags.

Both need **Manage Expressions**, for you and for the bot. The bot is not given that on the invite, so add it to the bot's role first.
