title: Running a giveaway
keywords: giveaway, giveaways, prize, draw, winner, reroll, contest, raffle
---
Members enter a giveaway by reacting to its message, and winners are drawn when it ends.

- `/giveaway start` starts one, `/giveaway end` ends it early, `/giveaway reroll` draws again and `/giveaway delete` removes it.
- On the dashboard, open your server and choose **Giveaways** under Entertainment, where you can do the same from one list.
