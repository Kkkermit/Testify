{bot} keeps only what a feature needs. Messages in servers are never stored.

### What is stored
- Server settings, such as the prefix and which features are on.
- Per-server records for features that are switched on: levels, balances, items and warnings.
- A dashboard session while you are signed in.
- A direct message you send the bot, if the owner has turned on the DM log, so they can reply.

### What leaves the bot
- A music search goes to YouTube or SoundCloud, and a translation to Google Translate.
- If the bot's owners have set up usage analytics, which commands, buttons, menus and forms you use go to a service they run, never what you type. `/privacy info` says whether your user ID goes with them.
- Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

### Removing your data
- `/delete-my-data` in Discord, or the Your data tab of [your account](/account) on the dashboard, deletes everything that is yours, in every server, at once. Only you see it, it asks for your username first, and it cannot be undone.
- Warnings, cases and blocks against you are kept, because they protect the servers you are in. Only the bot's owners can add or lift a block.
- `/profile delete` removes only your public profile, and `/economy delete` only your account in one server.
- `/privacy opt-out` stops the usage analytics counting you, and `/privacy erase` also deletes what they already hold. Deleting your data keeps the opt-out, so you are not counted again.

The [privacy notice](/privacy) lists everything that is stored and for how long.
