`/work` pays out, then you wait {fact:cooldown.work} before working again.

### Getting a job
Open `/shop` and choose **Jobs**. Each job lists its pay and what it needs. Some, such as programmer and doctor, need a laptop from the shop first.

Without a job, `/work` finds you odd jobs paying {fact:economy.workMin} to {fact:economy.workMax}.

> **Tip:** `/economy-info account` shows your current job.
