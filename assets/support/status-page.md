The [status page](/status) shows how the bot is doing right now and over the last 30 days.

- **The headline** says whether everything is working, partly working or down.
- **Uptime and speed**: how long it has been online, and how quickly it reaches Discord and its database.
- **History**: uptime for each of the last 30 days, and the last day in half-hour steps.
- **Services**: the outside services it relies on, such as Discord and YouTube.

In Discord, `/ping` shows the bot's latency and `/bot uptime` how long it has been running.

> **Tip:** If the page itself will not load, the bot is offline, and only whoever hosts it can start it again.
