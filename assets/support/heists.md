A heist is a group job. Everybody who joins puts in the same stake.

1. Run `/heist`, optionally with a stake. You need that much in your wallet.
2. Other members join with the button within {fact:economy.heistJoin}.
3. It needs {fact:economy.heistMin} to {fact:economy.heistMax} people, and goes ahead once the time is up.

After starting one, you wait {fact:cooldown.heist} before the next.
