A good order for setting up {bot} in a new server.

### Checklist
1. **Add the bot**, then drag its role near the top of **Server Settings → Roles** in Discord so it can manage the roles it hands out.
2. **Pick a prefix**, or keep `{prefix}`. Open your server's **Settings** on the dashboard, or run `/prefix`.
3. **Greet people** with a welcome message and an image card.
4. **Protect the server** with verification, AutoMod filters, link filtering and an audit log.
5. **Reward activity** with levelling and level rewards.
6. **Give members a way to reach staff** with a ticket panel.
7. **Tell your members** about `/help` and `/ask`.

> **Tip:** Every feature can be set up from Discord or from the dashboard, and both change the same settings.
