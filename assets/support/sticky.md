title: Sticky messages
keywords: sticky, sticky message, pinned message, keep message at bottom, pin to bottom
---
A sticky message stays at the bottom of a channel: when somebody posts, the bot reposts it underneath.

- In Discord, `/sticky-message setup` creates one, `/sticky-message check` shows it and `/sticky-message disable` removes it.
- On the dashboard, open your server and choose **Sticky** under Channels.
