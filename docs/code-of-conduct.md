# Code of conduct

Testify is a small open-source project. This is short on purpose — it is the standard everybody here is held
to, not a legal document.

## The standard

**Be decent to people.** Assume the person you are replying to is trying to help, is doing this in their spare
time, and may not share your first language or your level of experience.

Things that are welcome here:

- Asking a question that seems obvious. It probably is not, and if it is, the documentation needs fixing.
- Disagreeing with a decision, and saying why.
- Pull requests that are not finished, marked as drafts.
- Saying you got something wrong.

Things that are not:

- Personal attacks, insults, or comments about somebody's identity — including their gender, sexuality, race,
  nationality, religion, disability or age.
- Sexual language or imagery, anywhere in the project or its issues.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private. Continuing to contact somebody who has asked you to stop is
  harassment.
- Publishing somebody's private information.
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions to make a point, or bad-faith argument.

## Where this applies

Anywhere the project happens — issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, the Discord server, and
anywhere somebody is representing the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer, [@Kkkermit](https://github.com/Kkkermit), by opening a private security advisory on
the repository or by direct message on [Discord](https://discord.gg/xcMVwAVjSD). Do not open a public issue
about somebody's conduct.

A report will be read and answered. What happens next depends on what happened — usually a private word, and for
anything serious or repeated, removal from the project's spaces. Whoever reports something is not obliged to
justify why it bothered them.

If the person you need to report is the maintainer, say so in the report to Discord; there is no better answer
than that on a project this size, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.

## Where this came from

The substance follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), rewritten to be shorter
and to name a real reporting route rather than a placeholder email address.
